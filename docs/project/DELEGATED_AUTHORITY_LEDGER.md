## 1. Purpose

This ledger is the **single inventory** of:

1. **Active grants** — time-bounded or standing approvals the Chief Architect / Product Owner has delegated to the operating agent.  
2. **Usage log** — each concrete exercise of a grant (what was done, under which grant ID, charter check).  

**How to read this file**

- To see *what you authorized*: §3 Active Grants.  
- To see *what was used*: §4 Usage Log (newest first).  
- Unused / expired grants remain listed with status `Expired` or `Revoked`; they do not authorize new work.

If continuous autonomous development under the Constitution is granted, every material action that would otherwise have required a fresh cue **must** append a Usage Log row before or immediately after the work lands.
