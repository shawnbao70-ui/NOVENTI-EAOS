## Mission

Establish the NOVENTI Research Institute (NRI) as the permanent research organization of the NOVENTI Enterprise AI Operating System (EAOS).

The mission of NRI is to continuously discover, study, validate and model the future evolution of intelligent enterprises.

**Research is a permanent product of NOVENTI.**  
Every validated research result becomes a long-term strategic asset.

## Position in EAOS Governance

```text
EAOS Constitution
        ↓
Phoenix Governance
        ↓
NRI Research Governance   ← this Charter
        ↓
Research Programs
        ↓
Blueprint
        ↓
Architecture Decisions
        ↓
Implementation
```

Research never bypasses this governance hierarchy.

### Clarification of Dual Constitution Relationship

| Relationship | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| Constitution **governs** NRI | NRI may not violate constitutional invariants while researching |
| Research may **propose** Constitution change | Only via validated promotion → Constitution Review; NRI never edits Constitution directly |

```text
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
```

**Hard rule:** No capability may directly enter Blueprint, Constitution, or Implementation without validation.

Possible promotion targets:

```text

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
```

**Promotion is optional.**  
Research may remain permanently as Research Assets in the Research Library.

## Research Library

NRI maintains a permanent research repository at `docs/research/`.

Every research document shall contain:

| Field | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Research ID | Stable document ID (e.g. `NRI-RP-001-EDF`) |
| Version | Research semantic version |
| Status | Lifecycle / library status |
| Objective | Why the artifact exists |
| Scope | In / out of scope |
| Author | Primary author |
| Reviewer | Non-author reviewer when past Idea |
| Approval | Approval record or `Pending` |
| Dependencies | Upstream research / evidence deps |
| Related Capability | Capability targets if any |
| Related Blueprint | BP-* candidates (read-only refs) |
| Related Constitution | BOOK candidates (read-only refs) |
| Related ADR | ADR candidates / constraints |
| Promotion Status | Current promotion target/state |

See [RESEARCH_LIBRARY.md](RESEARCH_LIBRARY.md) for the registry.

## Restrictions

Research shall **not** directly modify:

- Runtime  
- Kernel  
- Source Code  
- Database  
- Constitution  
- Blueprint  
- Implementation  

Research produces **recommendations only**.  
Implementation requires separate approval under Phoenix Governance.
