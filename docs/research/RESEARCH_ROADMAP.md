## Purpose

Define the Generation-1 research sequence for continuous enterprise evolution study in the AI era, under the permanent Research Governance Charter.

## Strategic Intent

EAOS Foundation has reached architectural maturity. Future capabilities must originate from enterprise research, not from opportunistic implementation.

Phoenix operates **Dual-Track Governance** ([ADR-0162](../decisions/ADR-0162-dual-track-governance.md)): Engineering Track owns production truth; this Research Roadmap owns the Research Track sequence. No capability enters Blueprint or Constitution without Validation + Architecture Review.

This roadmap sequences research so that discovery, workforce, and evolution models form a coherent evidence base before any Blueprint or Constitution change is proposed.

## Non-Goals for This Roadmap

- No Kernel, Runtime, Smart Terminal, Brain, or Marketplace code changes
- No Constitution or Blueprint edits
- No product release commitments
- No Marketplace commercial policy resolution (remains engineering/legal track)

## Success Metrics for Generation-1

1. All ten programs have complete 17-section briefs.
2. Three first-wave frameworks exist and are internally consistent.
3. Promotion path from Idea → Architecture Review is documented and enforceable.
4. Zero research artifacts bypass Validation Rules.
5. Architecture Review Board can reject premature Constitution/Blueprint proposals by citing NRI stage.
