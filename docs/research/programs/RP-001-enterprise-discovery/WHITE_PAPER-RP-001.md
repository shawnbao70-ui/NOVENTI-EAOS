## Abstract

Enterprises fail AI and operating-model programs less from “insufficient models” than from **undiscovered enterprise truth**. The Enterprise Discovery Framework (EDF) defines how EAOS should learn an enterprise before advising transformation: durable identity (Profile, DNA), operating truth (Capabilities, Organization, Knowledge), readiness (AI, Automation, Infrastructure), and trajectory (Growth Stage, Evolution Potential, AI Roadmap). Discovery produces versioned dossiers for advisory evolution—not automatic execution. Cap≠Org, multi-dimensional readiness, and knowledge-authority rules are first-class. Peer review (臻宇) passed PR-01…12; this White Paper Draft synthesizes the Research Draft for freeze and later WP Acceptance. Live T3 evidence remains planned; synthetic WT-01…03 satisfy the desk walkthrough floor.

## 1. Problem Statement

Industry discovery is often license theater, org-chart theater, or stage labels without evidence. Downstream AI staffing and evolution advice then optimize the wrong objects. EAOS needs a governed, falsifiable discovery spine before Evolution Engine recommendations.
