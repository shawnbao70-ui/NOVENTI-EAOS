# RP-001 Custody Plan

**Program:** Enterprise Discovery  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Controls:** [DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK](../../../templates/DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK.md) · [CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY](../../../templates/CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY.md)

## Scope

Controls source inventories, worksheets, dossier versions, claim/source traces, facilitator records, and restricted handles produced under the [SITE_PLAN](SITE_PLAN.md) and [FIELD_KIT](FIELD_KIT.md).

## Custody nodes

1. **Enterprise source:** source owners retain original process, profile, capability, organization, knowledge, and readiness records.
2. **Authorized collection:** collector registers minimum redacted extracts/handles and timestamps against the LC ID.
3. **Secure intake:** evidence custodian validates classification, scope, source identity, integrity marker, and consent/access basis.
4. **Dossier workspace:** worksheets and dossier derivatives are versioned; each statement traces to artifact IDs.
5. **Redaction/review:** reviewer checks personal, tenant, strategy, and confidential content before registrar access.
6. **Registrar/disposition:** registrar resolves handles; custodian archives safe provenance and returns/deletes temporary extracts.

## Responsibility roles

1. Source owners validate original records and approved export scope.
2. Collector records context, timestamps, filters, and direct observation without expanding scope.
3. Evidence custodian controls storage, access, transfer, retention, and revocation.
4. Redaction/provenance reviewer validates dossier derivatives and claim/source trace.
5. Registrar assesses reviewability independently; custody receipt is not acceptance.

## Retention rules

1. Raw enterprise records remain with source owners; research stores handles unless an approved extract is necessary.
2. Temporary redacted extracts/working notes expire at the earlier of stated access expiry or post-verification review; renewal requires documented necessity.
3. Safe dossier versions, manifests, consent/access, and provenance retain according to the research record schedule with periodic access review.
4. Withdrawn/out-of-scope data is quarantined then returned/deleted; legal hold pauses only the specified items.

## Leakage response

1. Stop collection/sharing; isolate dossier workspace, links, and temporary exports.
2. Notify source owner, custodian, privacy/security/site contacts and preserve access logs.
3. Identify affected fields, recipients, caches/backups, dossier claims, and derived versions.
4. Revoke access, redact/rebuild derivatives, verify return/deletion, and mark affected evidence excluded until dispute review.
