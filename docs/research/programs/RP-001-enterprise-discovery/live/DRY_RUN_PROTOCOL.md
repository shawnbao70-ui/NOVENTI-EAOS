# RP-001 Dry-Run Protocol

**Mode:** synthetic  
**Tier:** T1 only  
**Registry effect:** none — RP-001 remains **Open**  

Purpose: rehearse Enterprise Discovery collection and registration without a live site, tenant, cohort, or observer. Follow the [live/synthetic fence](../../../templates/LIVE_VS_SYNTHETIC_FENCE.md).

## Desktop exercise

1. Allocate `DR-YYYYMMDD-RP-001-##`; list participants as role-players, not observers.
2. Create a fictitious enterprise covering selected discovery domains and deliberate source conflicts/gaps.
3. Run Sense → Structure → Score → Dossier → Advise using synthetic worksheets.
4. Check separation of Profile, DNA, Capability, Organization, readiness, Knowledge, Growth Stage, Evolution Potential, and roadmap.
5. Trace every dossier statement to a fixture; record unsupported claims and falsifiers.
6. Rehearse artifact-manifest, access, redaction, and registrar checks without creating an LC record.
7. Record protocol gaps and close as `Dry-run closed — T1`.

## Fake-data boundary

- Use generated organizations, people, documents, metrics, and non-routable identifiers only.
- No real dossier, tenant export, personal data, credential, production screenshot, or confidential source.
- Mark every artifact `SYNTHETIC / NOT LIVE EVIDENCE` and keep a fixture manifest.

## Prohibited outcome

Never mark this exercise, its artifacts, observer fields, or the Registry as Complete. Role-play, realism, peer review, or repeated runs do not raise T1 to T2/T3.

## Exit conditions

- Steps and fail-closed checks were exercised; gaps, owners, and fixture disposition are recorded.
- If real data or an actual field observation appears, stop and isolate it; begin a separately authorized LC intake.
- Registry remains **0 Complete** and RP-001 remains **Open**.
