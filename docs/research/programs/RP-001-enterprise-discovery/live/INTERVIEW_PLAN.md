# RP-001 Interview Plan

**Program:** Enterprise Discovery  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Elicit how real enterprise facts are sourced, reconciled, scored, and converted into a Discovery Dossier. Statements locate evidence; they do not replace operational artifacts.

## Interviewee roles

1. Enterprise/domain owner responsible for the selected scope.
2. Discovery facilitator or assessment lead.
3. Capability/process/knowledge source custodian.
4. AI/automation/infrastructure readiness representative.
5. Participant affected by the resulting dossier/roadmap.

Roles are sampling targets, not invented people.

## Core questions

1. Walk through the most recent discovery exercise from trigger to dossier delivery.
2. Which sources establish profile, process, capability, organization, knowledge, and readiness facts?
3. Where do sources conflict, and who records rather than suppresses the conflict?
4. How are Organization and Capability kept separate in workshops and outputs?
5. Which scores are directly evidenced, inferred, or unavailable?
6. What data or participant groups are commonly missing from a dossier?
7. How are AI-assisted notes/analysis reviewed, corrected, and traced?
8. Describe a case where discovery should produce HOLD or no recommendation.
9. What would falsify the dossier’s maturity/evolution conclusions?
10. Which versioned artifacts can a registrar inspect, and who controls access/retention?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask for passwords, unrestricted tenant exports, personal records, or unrelated confidential strategy.
2. Do not ask participants to endorse a maturity score, roadmap, Promote, Eng action, or Const/BP change.
3. Do not use blame framing such as “which department caused the failure?”
4. Do not imply that a title, score, or interview answer authorizes permissions/grants.

## Output mapping

1. Recent-exercise narrative → dated observation timeline in the capture/log.
2. Source inventory answers → artifact manifest and custodian/access fields.
3. Conflict/missing-data answers → gap, contradiction, and falsifier log.
4. Org≠Capability answers → invariant evidence and boundary notes.
5. AI-assisted review answers → provenance/correction audit.
6. Inspectable-artifact answers → custody requests and claim-to-source matrix.

## Bias and follow-up

Sample beyond the sponsor; compare facilitator claims with source custodians and affected participants. Record interview status, consent, role gaps, hearsay, and disconfirming accounts.
