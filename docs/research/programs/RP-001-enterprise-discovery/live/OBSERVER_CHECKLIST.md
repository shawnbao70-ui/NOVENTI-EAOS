# RP-001 Observer Checklist

**Program:** Enterprise Discovery  
**Preparation status:** **Open**  
**Assigned observer:** none  

Use this checklist only to prepare a real live capture. Record an observer name only after that person has agreed to attend; roles below are requirements, not invented identities.

## Observer roles

- [ ] Lead observer can distinguish source facts, participant statements, facilitator inference, and research scoring.
- [ ] Domain representative can validate the named enterprise scope and source ownership.
- [ ] Independent witness is identified for T3 when applicable, or an exception is documented for registrar review.
- [ ] Observer understands Org≠Capability and that discovery scores never authorize actions or grants.

## Site / system access

- [ ] Access approval covers the named site/tenant/cohort and dated observation window.
- [ ] Read-only access or supervised export is available for agreed profile, process, knowledge, capability, and readiness sources.
- [ ] Restricted systems have a custodian and controlled retrieval path; credentials are never copied into research Markdown.
- [ ] Recording, interview, and workshop consent rules are confirmed.

## Minimum artifacts

- [ ] Dated session/workshop record and scoped cohort description.
- [ ] Redacted source inventory and completed discovery worksheets.
- [ ] Versioned Discovery Dossier and claim-to-source trace.
- [ ] Gap/conflict/falsifier log plus provenance, retention, and access metadata.

## Ethics / confidentiality

- [ ] Collect only necessary enterprise and participant data; redact personal, tenant, security, and commercially sensitive content.
- [ ] Separate observed evidence from facilitator interpretation and disclose uncertainty.
- [ ] Do not pressure participants to validate a desired maturity score or roadmap.
- [ ] Store restricted raw material outside the repository and register only safe metadata/handles.

## Honest state

RP-001 remains **Open** with **0 Complete** live captures. This checklist is readiness material, not evidence, Promote, Eng ingest, or a floor change.
