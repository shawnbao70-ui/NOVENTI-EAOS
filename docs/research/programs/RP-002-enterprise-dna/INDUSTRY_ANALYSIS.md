## 1. Thesis

Enterprises mis-predict AI/automation fit when they use **personality/culture decks** or **stage labels** as constraints. Industry needs a slow-moving, evidence-linked **DNA vector** that explains HOLD vs Prefer without becoming HR discrimination or Permission input.
