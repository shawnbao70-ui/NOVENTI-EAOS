## Abstract

Similar tech stacks evolve differently because durable enterprise traits constrain which paths are plausible. The Enterprise DNA Model (EDNA) deepens RP-001’s eight axes (DX-01…08) into a measurable, versioned constraint vector: Decision Gravity, Exception Density, Formality Preference, Knowledge Stickiness, Asset Intensity, Compliance Reflex, Partner Embeddedness, and Change Absorption. DNA predicts fit and HOLD pressure for RP-007 advice; it never grants Permission and must not feed Runtime authorization. Synthetic scorecards SC-01…03 (T1) exercise the method across manufacturing, services, and stage-contrast dossiers. Peer 臻宇 passed PR-DNA-01…12. This White Paper Draft freezes EDNA for later WP Acceptance; Architecture Review and DNA→grant remain closed.

## 1. Problem Statement

Culture decks and single “maturity scores” either ignore constraints or weaponize assessment. Evolution engines without DNA over-recommend automation; vendors assume false formality. EAOS needs a falsifiable, non-authorizing trait model.

## 2. Research Questions

1. Are eight axes jointly useful without collapsing to one DNA IQ?  
2. Can DNA constrain RP-007 HOLD/fit without becoming authorization input?  
3. What falsifiers (retest instability, culture-quiz collapse, discrimination misuse) Hold WP Acceptance?
