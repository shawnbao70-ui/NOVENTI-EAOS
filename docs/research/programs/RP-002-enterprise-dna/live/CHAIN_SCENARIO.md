# RP-002 Commercial Chain Scenario

**Program:** Enterprise DNA  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Live chain/observer assigned:** none / none  
**Protocol:** [COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION](../../../templates/COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION.md) · **Terminal lens:** [TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD](../../../templates/TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD.md)

## Research purpose

Observe whether durable enterprise traits explain how the commercial chain handles evidence, exceptions, approval, customer commitments, and change—without turning DNA into individual profiling or authorization.

## Chain observation points

1. **Sample:** formal versus relational request intake, specification discipline, and exception tolerance.
2. **Quote:** pricing/approval centralization, evidence expectations, negotiation behavior, and version control.
3. **Order:** contract/credit/compliance formality, acceptance authority, and handling of ambiguity.
4. **Shipment:** planning horizon, coordination style, substitution/hold behavior, and operational resilience.
5. **Receipt/invoice:** dispute transparency, accountability, and reconciliation discipline.
6. **Payment:** risk posture, control formality, exception escalation, and learning from failures.
7. **Cross-stage:** stable traits versus system/process constraints and temporary context.
8. **Retest:** whether observed patterns persist across a later authorized chain/window.

## RP model mapping

1. Repeated chain behaviors supply evidence candidates for DX-01…08, not scores by anecdote.
2. Stage variation tests trait orthogonality and context sensitivity.
3. Baseline/follow-up chains support test/retest stability.
4. Exception/HOLD outcomes test recommendation constraint value.
5. Contradictory stages become confounders/limitations rather than forced trait labels.

## HARD HOLD / prohibited zones

1. HARD HOLD if one transaction or interview is generalized into enterprise DNA without corroboration.
2. HARD HOLD on worker/customer profiling, discriminatory use, or collection of unnecessary personal/payment data.
3. HARD HOLD if DNA influences credit, approval, Permission, grant, or Twin authorization.
4. HARD HOLD on transaction action, Brain execute, Promote/Eng instruction, or Const/BP change.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated/tokenized chain observation and context record.
2. Stage-level behavior/evidence matrix linked to DX criteria.
3. Dossier/instrument/scorecard versions and rater rationale.
4. Cross-stage contradiction/confounder log.
5. Baseline/follow-up chain comparison when authorized.
6. Recommendation-fit/HOLD and falsifier evidence.
7. Custody, consent, minimization, integrity, and retention records.

## Terminal research lens

A research card may visualize trait hypotheses beside stage evidence and confidence. It must label uncertainty, offer no automated score-to-action path, and remain isolated/read-only.
