# RP-002 Custody Plan

**Program:** Enterprise DNA  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Controls:** [DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK](../../../templates/DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK.md) · [CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY](../../../templates/CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY.md)

## Scope

Controls live dossier references, baseline/follow-up scorecards, rater records, delta/confounder analysis, recommendation-fit evidence, and provenance defined by the [SITE_PLAN](SITE_PLAN.md) and [FIELD_KIT](FIELD_KIT.md).

## Custody nodes

1. **Dossier source:** RP-001/source custodian retains original enterprise records and supplies controlled versions.
2. **Baseline collection:** collector registers minimum evidence and independent scorecards against a frozen instrument/version.
3. **Secure intake:** custodian separates identities from scoring records and validates consent, classification, and integrity.
4. **Follow-up collection:** new dated versions link to baseline without overwriting prior scores.
5. **Comparison/redaction:** reviewer creates inter-rater/delta/confounder derivatives with parent links and privacy checks.
6. **Registrar/disposition:** registrar reviews controlled versions; custodian archives safe provenance and disposes temporary sensitive extracts.

## Responsibility roles

1. Dossier/source owner validates source version and approved use.
2. Measurement collector records evidence and scores without altering the instrument.
3. Evidence custodian controls identity mapping, storage, access, transfer, and retention.
4. Measurement/privacy reviewer validates rater independence, transformations, and de-identification.
5. Registrar assesses tier evidence and excludes disputed or inaccessible versions.

## Retention rules

1. Identity keys and raw survey/interview material remain separated and expire as early as the approved measurement purpose allows.
2. Baseline material remains only through the authorized follow-up/comparison window unless a documented research necessity renews it.
3. Redacted scorecards, delta records, manifests, consent, and provenance follow the research record schedule with periodic necessity review.
4. Withdrawal, discriminatory-use risk, or unresolved provenance triggers quarantine and scoped return/deletion; legal hold is item-specific.

## Leakage response

1. Stop scoring/transfer and isolate identity keys, scorecards, and dossier handles.
2. Notify source owner, custodian, privacy/ethics/security contacts and preserve access history.
3. Determine exposed identities, traits, raters, derived recommendations, copies, and backups.
4. Revoke access, re-tokenize/redact or destroy affected derivatives, notify as required, and exclude evidence pending review.
