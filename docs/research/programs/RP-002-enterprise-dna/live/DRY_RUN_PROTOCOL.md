# RP-002 Dry-Run Protocol

**Mode:** synthetic  
**Tier:** T1 only  
**Registry effect:** none — RP-002 remains **Open**  

Purpose: rehearse DNA measurement, retest, and intake mechanics without live enterprise evidence. Apply the [live/synthetic fence](../../../templates/LIVE_VS_SYNTHETIC_FENCE.md).

## Desktop exercise

1. Allocate `DR-YYYYMMDD-RP-002-##`; all participants are role-players.
2. Generate a fictitious RP-001 dossier with DX-01…08 source cues, ambiguity, and missing values.
3. Have two role-players score independently; compare rationale and inter-rater differences.
4. Generate a synthetic +90-day dossier and rehearse test/retest and confounder analysis.
5. Test whether DNA constraints alter a recommendation/HOLD without entering HR, permission, or grant logic.
6. Rehearse manifest, provenance, rejection, and resubmission records.
7. Record gaps and close as `Dry-run closed — T1`.

## Fake-data boundary

- All dossiers, traits, respondents, dates, scores, and outcomes are generated.
- No real employee, culture survey, customer dossier, HR record, or tenant identifier.
- Mark outputs `SYNTHETIC / NOT LIVE EVIDENCE`; document fixture generation and transformations.

## Prohibited outcome

No dry-run scorecard or simulated retest may be marked Complete or relabeled T2/T3. A real facilitator or reviewer does not make generated evidence live.

## Exit conditions

- Measurement and retest paths, gaps, and fail-closed DNA≠grant checks are recorded.
- Unexpected real data triggers immediate stop/isolation and a separate authorized LC process.
- Registry remains **0 Complete** and RP-002 remains **Open**.
