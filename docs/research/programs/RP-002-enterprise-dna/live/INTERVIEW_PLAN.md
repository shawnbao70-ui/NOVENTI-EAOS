# RP-002 Interview Plan

**Program:** Enterprise DNA  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Understand evidence-backed interpretation, rater variation, retest stability, and constraint value of DX-01…08 without profiling individuals or creating authorization semantics.

## Interviewee roles

1. Enterprise representative familiar with baseline and follow-up context.
2. DNA measurement facilitator/rater.
3. Independent measurement or methods reviewer.
4. RP-001 dossier/source custodian.
5. Recommendation/HOLD consumer able to discuss fit.

## Core questions

1. Which dossier evidence informed each DNA axis at baseline?
2. Where did raters disagree, and what evidence—not hierarchy—resolved or preserved disagreement?
3. Which axis definitions were ambiguous in this enterprise context?
4. What material enterprise changes occurred between baseline and follow-up?
5. Which score changes reflect evidence versus instrument/rater drift?
6. How are missing evidence and low confidence represented?
7. Describe a recommendation that improved, worsened, or became HOLD after DNA constraints.
8. What alternative explanation could account for the observed recommendation fit?
9. What would falsify claimed trait stability or orthogonality?
10. Where are scorecards, instrument versions, consent, and provenance retained?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask interviewees to characterize named employees, protected groups, or “culture fit.”
2. Do not solicit HR, discipline, compensation, health, or performance data unrelated to the research claim.
3. Do not ask whether DNA scores should grant/revoke permission or authorize Twin.
4. Do not lead participants to defend prior scores or suppress context change.

## Output mapping

1. Axis-source answers → scorecard-to-dossier trace.
2. Rater disagreement answers → inter-rater comparison and dissent record.
3. Context-change answers → baseline/follow-up confounder timeline.
4. Confidence/missing-evidence answers → limitations and gap log.
5. Recommendation-fit answers → fit/HOLD comparison and falsifiers.
6. Retention answers → custody manifest and retrieval test.

## Bias and follow-up

Keep raters’ accounts separate before reconciliation; seek a source custodian and recommendation consumer, not only model authors. Treat repeated opinion as statements, not corroborated artifacts.
