- [ ] Lead observer understands DX-01…08, test/retest design, and evidence-backed scoring.
- [ ] Enterprise representative can validate RP-001 dossier inputs and material context changes.

## Ethics / confidentiality

- [ ] Report uncertainty and alternative explanations; do not label culture from anecdote.

- [ ] Preserve prior measurements and correction history without retroactive score editing.
