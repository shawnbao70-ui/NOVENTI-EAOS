## Field objective

Capture repeated, comparable evidence of how SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR discrepancies are handled, so DNA trait hypotheses can be scored/held without profiling or authorization.

## Minimum field artifacts

1. Tokenized baseline SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR chain with source/version/time.
2. Follow-up chain/window using the same frozen measurement instrument.
3. Independent DX scorecards linked to exact chain evidence.
4. Inter-rater disagreement and reconciliation rationale.
5. Cross-stage/window confounder and material-context-change record.
6. Reconciliation cadence/owner/tolerance/exception evidence.
7. Recommendation-fit/HOLD outcome, falsifiers, custody, consent, and retention.

## Reconciliation questions

1. Is the observed response stable across stages/windows or local to one incident?
2. Which trait inference has direct evidence, and which is facilitator interpretation?
3. What confounder—system outage, staffing, policy, customer, site—could explain the pattern?
4. Does the trait improve recommendation fit or only redescribe the problem?
5. What evidence would reverse the score or require HOLD?
