## Observable reconciliation checkpoints

1. SO changes propagate consistently or depend on informal/manual correction.
2. DO release/partial/cancel behavior follows declared controls or relationship-based exceptions.
3. Inventory drift is measured, explained, tolerated, or hidden differently across sites/teams.
4. Receipt discrepancies are recorded/escalated or resolved outside governed evidence.
5. AR mismatches are reconciled on a stable cadence or only after external pressure.
6. Dual-write/interface retry errors produce repeatable response patterns and ownership.
7. Reconciliation/勾兑 absence persists across windows or is a temporary staffing/system incident.
8. Baseline and follow-up show stable or materially changed traits with confounders disclosed.
9. Exception/return/chargeback cases contradict the initial trait hypothesis.
