## 1. Thesis

Enterprises fund **departments and tool seats** faster than they model **what they can do**. Industry needs Cap≠Org graphs with dependency edges so AI staffing, automation affinity, and package buy decisions hit critical-path gaps — without treating capability presence as Permission.
