# RP-003 Dry-Run Protocol

**Mode:** synthetic  
**Tier:** T1 only  
**Registry effect:** none — RP-003 remains **Open**  

Purpose: rehearse capability-graph collection and comparison without live organizational evidence. Apply the [live/synthetic fence](../../../templates/LIVE_VS_SYNTHETIC_FENCE.md).

## Desktop exercise

1. Allocate `DR-YYYYMMDD-RP-003-##`; do not populate observer identities.
2. Generate a fictitious enterprise, departmental roadmap, outcomes, services, and dependencies.
3. Build a capability graph and rehearse L0–L4 maturity and A0–A4 automation-affinity scoring.
4. Introduce deliberate department/capability collisions and test Cap≠Org resolution.
5. Compare synthetic capability and departmental roadmaps using a declared rubric.
6. Verify no node, owner role, score, or roadmap item becomes permission or grant input.
7. Record protocol gaps and close as `Dry-run closed — T1`.

## Fake-data boundary

- Use generated graph nodes, owners, portfolios, outcomes, metrics, and dependencies.
- No production capability catalog, org chart, permission map, personal data, or confidential roadmap.
- Label graph exports and comparisons `SYNTHETIC / NOT LIVE EVIDENCE`.

## Prohibited outcome

The graph cannot be marked Complete or upgraded by realism, repeated use, or review. Aggregated synthetic graphs remain T1.

## Exit conditions

- Graph, comparison, provenance, and Capability≠Permission checks were rehearsed and gaps logged.
- Real source material triggers stop/isolation and a new authorized LC record.
- Registry remains **0 Complete** and RP-003 remains **Open**.
