# RP-003 Interview Plan

**Program:** Capability First  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Elicit how participants define, evidence, score, and use capabilities independently of organization structure and Permission.

## Interviewee roles

1. Capability mapping facilitator/modeler.
2. Domain/process/service owner.
3. Portfolio/strategy or outcome owner.
4. Organization reference custodian for comparison.
5. Roadmap consumer from AI/workforce/evolution planning.

## Core questions

1. Walk through how one capability was identified from outcomes and evidence.
2. Which criteria distinguish a capability from a department, process, service, or role?
3. How were dependencies and spanning capabilities validated?
4. What evidence supports the selected L0–L4 maturity rating?
5. What evidence supports A0–A4 automation affinity and risk limits?
6. Where did ownership or naming remain contested?
7. How did the capability roadmap differ from the departmental roadmap?
8. Which planning decision became clearer, less clear, or HOLD?
9. What would falsify a node, dependency, maturity score, or claimed outcome?
10. Which graph/source versions and comparison artifacts are independently retrievable?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask who “owns” a capability as a proxy for authority, individual performance, or permission.
2. Do not request confidential roadmap detail beyond the approved minimum.
3. Do not ask participants to map capability IDs directly to grants.
4. Do not frame department disagreement as obstruction or incompetence.

## Output mapping

1. Capability-definition answers → glossary/metamodel evidence.
2. Source/dependency answers → node/edge claim trace.
3. Maturity/affinity answers → scoring rationale and confidence record.
4. Contested mapping answers → dissent/ambiguity log.
5. Roadmap comparison answers → decision-clarity rubric.
6. Falsifier/version answers → artifact requests and custody manifest.

## Bias and follow-up

Balance modelers with domain and roadmap consumers; record department-power influence, terminology coaching, and missing operational voices. Validate claims against graph/source artifacts.
