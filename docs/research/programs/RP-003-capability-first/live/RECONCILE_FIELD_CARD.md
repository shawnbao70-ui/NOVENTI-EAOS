## Field objective

Capture evidence for reconciliation capabilities and dependencies across SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR, while keeping Capability separate from Organization, Permission, and execution.

## Minimum field artifacts

1. Tokenized end-to-end chain observation and outcome inventory.
2. Source/state/version evidence for each SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR fact.
3. Candidate capability graph with stage-to-node/dependency trace.
4. Reconciliation owner-role, cadence, tolerance, service-level, and exception evidence.
5. L0–L4 maturity rationale backed by observed controls/outcomes.
6. A0–A4 automation-affinity rationale including partial/return/dispute cases.
7. Department-roadmap comparison, dissent, falsifiers, and custody record.

## Reconciliation questions

1. Is each candidate a capability outcome rather than a department, system, role, or state?
2. Which dependency fails when facts drift or reconciliation is absent?
3. What evidence supports maturity beyond a happy-path walkthrough?
4. Which exceptions require human/control supervision and reduce automation affinity?
5. Does ownership describe accountability without implying Permission/grant?

## HARD HOLD

1. No department/system/title copied as capability without outcome evidence.
2. No Capability/owner→Permission/grant or transaction authority.
3. No Promote, floor change, Eng ingest, Brain execute, Twin authorize, or CRUD.
4. No knowledge/Const/BP/Kernel/API/UI/code or operational-record change.
