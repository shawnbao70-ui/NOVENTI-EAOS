## 1. Thesis

Global enterprises fail when platforms assume **one org ideology** (classic manager hierarchy). Industry needs decision-rights-parameterized discovery, capability, workforce, and evolution advice that treat matrix, partnership, shop-floor, council, and multi-entity forms as first-class — without Org-shape→Permission.
