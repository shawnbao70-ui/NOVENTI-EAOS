## Abstract

Enterprise advice and UX often assume classic manager hierarchy as the only legitimate form. The Organization Neutrality Model (ONM) asserts **org-form pluralism** (OF-01…07) and a neutrality checklist (N-01…08): Structure ≠ Capability and Structure ≠ Permission (`org_shape_grant: never`). Synthetic audits NA-01…02 (T1) apply the checklist across hierarchy+shop-floor and partnership+matrix forms with Cap ID stability. Peer 臻宇 passed PR-ON-01…12. This White Paper Draft freezes ONM for later WP Acceptance; Architecture Review and Org-shape→grant remain closed.

## 1. Problem Statement

Hierarchy chauvinism and “Level N org = mature” ladders punish matrix, council, multi-entity, and shop-floor forms. Manager-only UX and org-shape→grant pressure break Cap≠Org and Permission discipline. EAOS needs a falsifiable neutrality constraint thesis.

## 2. Research Questions

1. Can the same Cap instruments run across ≥2 org forms without Cap ID rewrite?  
2. Does N-01…08 catch hierarchy chauvinism and manager-only UX defects?  
3. What falsifiers (hierarchy-as-maturity, org_shape_grant) Hold WP Acceptance?
