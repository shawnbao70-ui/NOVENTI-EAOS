# RP-004 Commercial Chain Scenario

**Program:** Organization Neutrality  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Live chains/observers assigned:** none / none  
**Protocol:** [COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION](../../../templates/COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION.md) · **Terminal lens:** [TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD](../../../templates/TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD.md)

## Research purpose

Compare the same commercial-chain instrument across distinct organization forms to identify hierarchy, manager, single-entity, or department assumptions without ranking organizations.

## Chain observation points

1. **Sample:** who may initiate/coordinate under hierarchy, matrix, council, network, or shop-floor forms.
2. **Quote:** how pricing/terms decision rights and dual controls are represented.
3. **Order:** how acceptance, challenge, and accountability work across entities/forms.
4. **Shipment:** how fulfillment/safety/trade coordination crosses units without manager-only language.
5. **Receipt/invoice:** how dispute, proof, billing, and segregation are expressed.
6. **Payment:** how authorization, dual control, reconciliation, and escalation avoid title assumptions.
7. **Cross-stage:** capability ownership versus organization structure and Permission.
8. **Comparison:** forced-assumption defects and remediation across at least two contexts.

## RP model mapping

1. Stage decision rights test neutrality checklist constructs.
2. Cross-entity handoffs test organization-plural representation.
3. Capability/structure comparisons test Cap≠Org.
4. Approval language/UI evidence tests manager-only bias.
5. Re-applied remediation tests whether meaning survives across contexts.

## HARD HOLD / prohibited zones

1. HARD HOLD if contexts are ranked as superior/mature because of shape.
2. HARD HOLD on cross-entity raw-data sharing or disclosure without separate approval.
3. HARD HOLD if organization shape/title implies Permission, grant, or transaction authority.
4. HARD HOLD on transaction action, Brain execute, Twin authorize, Promote/Eng opening, or Const/BP rewrite.

## Required artifacts

1. Separate dated/tokenized chain logs for each context.
2. Frozen shared observation instrument/version.
3. Stage decision-right and capability-ownership matrices.
4. Forced-assumption defect excerpts with redaction.
5. Cross-context comparison matrix.
6. Remediation/retest record.
7. Segregated custody, access, consent, provenance, and falsifier records.

## Terminal research lens

A card may compare how the same evidence/HOLD state is expressed for different organization forms. It must not privilege one hierarchy or map role labels to grants.
