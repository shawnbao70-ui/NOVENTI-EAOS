# RP-004 Custody Plan

**Program:** Organization Neutrality  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Controls:** [DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK](../../../templates/DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK.md) · [CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY](../../../templates/CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY.md)

## Scope

Controls separately authorized organization-context profiles, shared instruments, neutrality audits, defect excerpts, comparison derivatives, and remediation/retest records under the [SITE_PLAN](SITE_PLAN.md) and [FIELD_KIT](FIELD_KIT.md).

## Custody nodes

1. **Context A source:** its owner retains original organization/decision-right/workflow evidence.
2. **Context B source:** its owner retains separately governed originals; no cross-entity raw transfer.
3. **Independent intake:** collectors register minimum redacted handles under context-specific access controls.
4. **Audit workspace:** each completed checklist remains segregated and tied to the frozen shared instrument.
5. **Comparison/redaction:** reviewer creates a de-identified cross-context derivative with parent links.
6. **Registrar/disposition:** registrar accesses permitted evidence; context exports expire separately and safe comparison provenance remains.

## Responsibility roles

1. Each context/source owner approves its evidence and sharing boundary.
2. Neutrality collector applies the same instrument and records deviations.
3. Evidence custodian enforces context segregation, access, transfers, and retention.
4. Neutrality/privacy reviewer validates de-identification, comparable transformations, and non-stereotyping.
5. Registrar reviews defects/remediation without broadening cross-entity access.

## Retention rules

1. Raw context artifacts stay in their originating stores and follow each owner’s shorter/stricter rule.
2. Temporary redacted excerpts expire after comparison/registrar review; they cannot be reused for unrelated benchmarking.
3. Shared instrument, safe defect taxonomy, comparison matrix, manifest, and provenance follow the research record schedule.
4. Consent withdrawal, entity-sharing conflict, or dispute quarantines only affected context/derivatives and triggers scoped disposition.

## Leakage response

1. Stop cross-context comparison/sharing and isolate affected workspace/handles.
2. Notify the relevant source owner(s), custodian, privacy/legal/security contacts without exposing the other context.
3. Trace leaked entity, decision-right, individual, cultural, and workflow details through derivatives/copies.
4. Revoke access, separate/rebuild comparisons, verify return/deletion, and mark affected evidence excluded.
