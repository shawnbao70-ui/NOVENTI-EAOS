# RP-004 Dry-Run Protocol

**Mode:** synthetic  
**Tier:** T1 only  
**Registry effect:** none — RP-004 remains **Open**  

Purpose: rehearse a comparative neutrality audit without real organizations or observers. Apply the [live/synthetic fence](../../../templates/LIVE_VS_SYNTHETIC_FENCE.md).

## Desktop exercise

1. Allocate `DR-YYYYMMDD-RP-004-##`; use role labels only.
2. Generate at least two contrasting fictitious organization forms with equivalent business instruments.
3. Apply the same neutrality checklist to both and log forced hierarchy/manager assumptions.
4. Rehearse decision-right and capability-ownership parameterization while preserving Structure≠Permission and Cap≠Org.
5. Record redacted-style examples, remediation proposals, exceptions, and falsifiers.
6. Test comparative artifact registration without naming real entities.
7. Close as `Dry-run closed — T1` with unresolved protocol gaps.

## Fake-data boundary

- Organization forms, councils, entities, workers, jurisdictions, and decisions are invented.
- Do not transform a real client or employer case merely by deleting its name.
- Label all profiles and audits `SYNTHETIC / NOT LIVE EVIDENCE`.

## Prohibited outcome

No synthetic neutrality audit may be marked Complete or used to assert a real organization/product passed. Multiple fictional cases do not create T2/T3.

## Exit conditions

- Comparative method and non-discrimination checks were exercised; gaps and owners recorded.
- Any recognizable real case or restricted language sample triggers stop/isolation and separate LC intake.
- Registry remains **0 Complete** and RP-004 remains **Open**.
