# RP-004 Interview Plan

**Program:** Organization Neutrality  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Identify forced organizational assumptions across at least two real contexts while respecting cultural, jurisdictional, labor, and entity boundaries.

## Interviewee roles

1. Representative from organization context A with direct process knowledge.
2. Representative from organization context B with equivalent scope.
3. Neutrality audit facilitator/reviewer.
4. Decision-right/workflow or governance custodian.
5. Affected user/participant encountering the instrument language.

## Core questions

1. Which instrument terms or flows assume a manager, hierarchy, department, or single entity?
2. How are decisions initiated, challenged, approved, and audited in this context?
3. How is capability ownership represented separately from structure?
4. Which council, matrix, network, dual-control, or shop-floor patterns are difficult to express?
5. Did any score penalize an organization form rather than an actual capability/control gap?
6. Which language changed meaning across contexts or jurisdictions?
7. What forced-assumption defect affected a real workflow or participant?
8. Did the proposed remediation preserve meaning in both contexts?
9. What evidence would show the instrument is still non-neutral?
10. Which comparable audit artifacts may be reviewed without cross-entity disclosure?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask which organization form is “best,” “modern,” or more mature by default.
2. Do not solicit identifiable internal disputes, protected characteristics, or unrelated governance secrets.
3. Do not ask organization shape to imply permission, grant, legitimacy, or executive rank.
4. Do not reveal one context’s raw evidence to another without explicit authorization.

## Output mapping

1. Assumption/language answers → forced-defect inventory.
2. Decision-right answers → neutral representation examples.
3. Cross-context meaning answers → comparison matrix.
4. Scoring-bias answers → instrument defect/falsifier log.
5. Remediation answers → proposal and retest record.
6. Comparable-artifact answers → segregated custody/retrieval plan.

## Bias and follow-up

Use the same core guide across contexts; record translation, seniority, cultural interpretation, and sponsor influence. Do not resolve differences by majority or by privileging headquarters.
