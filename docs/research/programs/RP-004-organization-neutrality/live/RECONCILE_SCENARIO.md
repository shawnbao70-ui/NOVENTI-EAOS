# RP-004 Reconciliation Observation Scenario

**Program:** Organization Neutrality  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Assigned live contexts / observers:** none / none  

## Mapping theme

Observe how distinct organization forms hold and reconcile parallel **SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR** facts without forcing manager-only, hierarchy-only, or single-entity assumptions. RP-004 evaluates neutrality defects; it does not execute or redesign the organization.

| Fact family | Neutrality observation | Score / hold treatment |
|-------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| SO | Initiation/change decision rights across forms | HOLD if title/hierarchy is assumed |
| DO | Release/coordination under matrix/council/multi-entity patterns | Score representational neutrality |
| Inventory | Site/entity ownership and shared truth boundaries | HOLD on forced centralization |
| Receipt | Acknowledgment/dispute accountability without manager-only flow | Preserve plural governance |
| AR | Dual control/reconciliation/escalation across entities | HOLD if shape implies authority |

## RP-004 model treatment

1. Identical reconciliation instruments are applied in comparable contexts.
2. Decision rights and capability ownership remain separate from organization structure.
3. Forced assumptions are tied to exact fields/language/flows.
4. Remediation is retested across contexts.
5. No organization form is ranked or converted into Permission/grant.

## Observable reconciliation checkpoints

1. SO correction authority cannot be represented without a “manager” field.
2. DO release spans matrix/council/entities but tooling assumes one hierarchy.
3. Inventory ownership across sites/entities is collapsed into one organizational unit.
4. Receipt acknowledgment requires a title that does not exist in the observed form.
5. AR reconciliation/dual control works differently across entities but one path is treated as default.
6. Dual-write responsibility is assigned by department rather than decision right/capability.
7. Reconciliation/勾兑 absence is blamed on shape instead of missing capability/control.
8. Exception/dispute escalation excludes councils, peers, dual control, or shop-floor roles.
9. Proposed remediation fixes one context but introduces defects in another.

## Required live evidence

1. Comparable dated/tokenized reconciliation observations from at least two contexts.
2. Frozen shared instrument and context-specific role/decision-right maps.
3. SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR approval/control evidence for each context.
4. Forced-assumption defect examples and source versions.
5. Remediation/retest and unresolved-dissent evidence.
6. Real context representatives corroborated by artifacts.
7. Segregated custody, consent, minimization, retention, and falsifiers.

Without these, RP-004 remains **Open / 0 Complete**.
