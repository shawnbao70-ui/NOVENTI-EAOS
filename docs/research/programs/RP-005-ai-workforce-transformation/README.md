## 1. Research Objective

Research every enterprise role family; determine Human, AI, Robot, and Device responsibilities; capability mapping; fusion opportunities; risk separation; legal constraints. Produce AI-Native Role Framework (ANRF).

## 2. Business Background

Enterprises add AI tools without redesigning roles, creating shadow automation and unclear accountability. BOOK03 defines AI taxonomy; research translates it into role redesign practice for humans, robots, and devices.

## 3. Industry Problems

Job descriptions ignore AI co-workers; “AI replaces role” skips residual human duty; robots/devices/agents conflated; permissions follow titles; legal duty verbally assigned to models; informal fusion fails audits.

## 4. Future Trends

| Trend | Confidence | Horizon |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Human+AI+Robot role compositions | High | 1–4 years |
| Regulatory accountability maps | High | 1–3 years |
| Device/edge actors in workforce models | High | 2–5 years |
| Capability-based staffing | Medium | 3–6 years |

## 5. Enterprise Value

Safer AI/robot deployment; clearer compliance posture; higher automation ROI; reduced informal AI surprise; Evolution Engine workforce inputs.

## 6. Capability Model

Role → Outcomes → Capabilities → Duties (R1–R10) → Actor assignment → Risk class → Controls → Fusion eligibility.  
Workforce maturity: Assistive → Agentized → AI-Workforce → Adaptive cells (research constructs). Includes Robot Workforce and Autonomous Device duty patterns.

## 7. Architecture Impact

Potential role-composition models above Organization/Identity; ownership classification required before placement.

## 8. Kernel Impact

Identity/Organization/Permission/Workflow may later need composition metadata; role labels never grant permission.

## 9. Runtime Impact

AI Runtime remains sole AI execution path; action classes map to approval bridges.

## 10. Smart Terminal Impact

Duty dashboards, supervision UX, handoff surfaces—not role truth authority.

## 11. Enterprise Brain Impact

Advise fusion opportunities and risk hotspots; never assign identities/permissions.

## 12. Marketplace Impact

Role-pack templates; industry duty libraries; AI employee skill packages with declared permissions.

## 13. Developer Platform Impact

Later: role-pack SDKs, duty schema for package authors, extension patterns for supervision UIs—after promotion only.

## 14. Potential Blueprint Impact

AI, Smart Terminal, Package blueprint role-template sections.

## 15. Potential Constitutional Impact

BOOK03 refinements; legal/governance book linkages—proposal only.

## 16. Validation Requirements

V-AW-01…05; legal review of responsibility matrix; ≥2 enterprise role inventories; BOOK03 alignment check.

## 17. Enterprise Pilot Strategy

Office-heavy + operations-heavy enterprises; top 30 role classes; stress-test fusion with compliance observers.

## 18. Success Criteria

Major role families covered; legal coherence; fusion vetoes explicit; EAOS taxonomy alignment; RP-007 consumability.

## 19. Promotion Criteria

White Paper after legal peer pass; Capability Model when maturity bands measurable; Architecture Review at T3/T4 with zero responsibility-transfer defects; Remain Asset allowed.

## 20. Migration Strategy

Migrate from title-based staffing → duty/capability maps → staged fusion (F1→F2→physical loops) with soak periods. No mass AI “replacement” programs. Human R1/R2 duties migrate last (i.e., never away from humans).

## 21. Long-term Evolution

Continuous role re-fusion under Evolution Engine; robot/device workforce deepening with RP-008; developer role-pack ecosystem; permanent Research Asset lineage.
