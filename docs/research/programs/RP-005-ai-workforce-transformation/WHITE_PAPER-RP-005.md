## Abstract

Enterprises reorganize titles faster than they redesign responsibility. The AI-Native Role Framework (ANRF) models roles as compositions of Human, AI, Robot, and Device duties while preserving human legal and business residual accountability. Title ≠ Permission grant; AI is not a legal person; Digital Human is presentation, not a duty bearer; fusion never erases risk separation (RC0–RC7). Synthetic inventories RI-01…02 demonstrate Cap≠title and `auto_grant_minted: never`. Legal peer 包锦昱 passed PR-AW-01…12. This White Paper Draft synthesizes ANRF for freeze; WP content Acceptance and any Permission productization remain closed.

## 1. Problem Statement

AI staffing programs confuse org boxes with capabilities, mint informal “AI employee rights,” and pressure Runtime grants from titles. EAOS needs a role framework that is BOOK03-compatible and Dual-Track safe.

## 2. Research Questions

1. How are Human/AI/Robot/Device responsibilities composed without transferring legal duty?  
2. Which risk classes must Hold Assist/Agentize/Robotize?  
3. Can inventories prove title≠grant and non-personhood under legal peer review?
