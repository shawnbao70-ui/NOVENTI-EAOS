# RP-005 Commercial Chain Scenario

**Protocol:** [COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION](../../../templates/COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION.md) · **Terminal lens:** [TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD](../../../templates/TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD.md)

## Research purpose

Observe Human/AI/Robot/Device duties, supervision, handoffs, legal accountability, and fusion vetoes across the commercial chain without evaluating individuals or automating commitments.

## Chain observation points

1. **Sample:** Human/AI/device intake, product matching, physical sample handling, and accountable review.
2. **Quote:** AI assistance versus human pricing/terms approval and explanation.
3. **Order:** customer acceptance, compliance/credit duty, exception handling, and accountable commitment.
4. **Shipment:** Human/Robot/Device duties in allocation, pick/pack, safety, release, and logistics.
5. **Receipt/invoice:** proof, discrepancy, billing, tax, and adjustment duties.
6. **Payment:** Human/system duties in matching, fraud review, settlement, and exceptions.
7. **Cross-stage:** supervision, escalation, fallback, identity, and role-to-duty continuity.
8. **Failure:** shadow automation, unavailable actor, incorrect output, and residual human responsibility.

## RP model mapping

1. Stage outcomes/duties map to the ANRF responsibility matrix.
2. Human/AI/Robot/Device assignments test taxonomy clarity.
3. Commercial/legal commitments test residual R1/R2 human duties.
4. Exceptions/fallbacks map to risk classes, controls, and fusion vetoes.
5. Handoff/supervision evidence tests workforce maturity without role→grant.

## HARD HOLD / prohibited zones

1. HARD HOLD if Research or AI is asked to price, accept order, release shipment, invoice, or pay.
2. HARD HOLD if AI is treated as legal person or accountability sink.
3. HARD HOLD on worker surveillance, replacement targeting, retaliation, or unnecessary HR data.
4. HARD HOLD on Role/Title/Capability→grant, Brain execute, Twin authorize, Promote/Eng opening, or Const/BP change.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated/tokenized chain observation log.
2. Stage outcomes/duties/actor responsibility matrix.
3. Approval, supervision, escalation, and fallback evidence.
4. Risk/control/legal/safety and fusion-veto record.
5. Shadow-automation/exception/falsifier log.
6. Controlled chain-state/audit handles.
7. Consent, minimization, custody, integrity, and retention records.
