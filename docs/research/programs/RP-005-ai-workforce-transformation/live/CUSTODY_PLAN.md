# RP-005 Custody Plan

**Program:** AI Workforce Transformation  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Controls:** [DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK](../../../templates/DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK.md) · [CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY](../../../templates/CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY.md)

## Scope

Controls workforce role/process sources, duty matrices, supervision/handoff evidence, legal/safety reviews, fusion vetoes, and de-identified incident examples under the [SITE_PLAN](SITE_PLAN.md) and [FIELD_KIT](FIELD_KIT.md).

## Custody nodes

1. **HR/process/safety source:** authorized owners retain original worker, role, incident, and control records.
2. **Minimum collection:** collector registers role-class/aggregate extracts and separates participant identities.
3. **Secure intake:** custodian validates labor/privacy/legal/safety basis, classification, and identity-key separation.
4. **Duty-map workspace:** versioned matrices link to source IDs without copying unnecessary personal data.
5. **Legal/safety redaction:** reviewers approve responsibility/control/veto derivatives and residual-risk notes.
6. **Registrar/disposition:** registrar reviews controlled evidence; identity-bearing/temporary extracts are returned/deleted first.

## Responsibility roles

1. HR/process/safety source owners approve minimum scope and source versions.
2. Workforce collector records duties/handoffs without performance evaluation or retaliation use.
3. Evidence custodian controls identity keys, sensitive stores, access logs, transfer, and retention.
4. Legal/privacy/safety reviewer validates lawful/ethical handling and actor-accountability claims.
5. Registrar assesses reviewability independently; role/title never grants.

## Retention rules

1. Direct identifiers, raw interviews, and worker-level records use the shortest approved retention and stay separated from role matrices.
2. Temporary incident/handoff extracts expire after legal/safety and registrar review unless an item-specific hold applies.
3. De-identified duty/control/veto matrices, manifests, consent, and provenance follow the research record schedule.
4. Participant withdrawal, retaliation risk, or secondary-use request triggers quarantine and scoped return/deletion.

## Leakage response

1. Stop access/sharing and isolate identity maps, role records, interviews, and incident extracts.
2. Notify HR/source owner, custodian, privacy/legal/security/safety contacts under workforce incident procedures.
3. Determine affected workers, duties, incidents, controls, recipients, derivatives, and backup copies.
4. Revoke access, support required notification, re-de-identify/destroy derivatives, and exclude evidence pending review.
