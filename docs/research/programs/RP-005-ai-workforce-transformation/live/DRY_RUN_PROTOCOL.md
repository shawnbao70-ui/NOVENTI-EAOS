# RP-005 Dry-Run Protocol

**Mode:** synthetic  
**Tier:** T1 only  
**Registry effect:** none — RP-005 remains **Open**  

Purpose: rehearse Human/AI/Robot/Device duty mapping without live workforce data. Apply the [live/synthetic fence](../../../templates/LIVE_VS_SYNTHETIC_FENCE.md).

## Desktop exercise

1. Allocate `DR-YYYYMMDD-RP-005-##`; participants role-play domain, legal, safety, and observer roles.
2. Generate fictitious office and/or operations role families, tasks, handoffs, incidents, and controls.
3. Map Outcomes → Capabilities → Duties → Actor → Risk → Controls → Fusion eligibility.
4. Inject ambiguous accountability and test residual human R1/R2 duties, supervision, escalation, and vetoes.
5. Verify AI is not a legal person and Title/Role/Capability never mints grants.
6. Rehearse redaction, manifest, legal-review, exception, and rejection records.
7. Close as `Dry-run closed — T1`.

## Fake-data boundary

- Use generated workers, roles, schedules, incidents, performance indicators, and legal scenarios.
- No HR file, employee identity, labor record, safety incident, production payload, or real job evaluation.
- Mark every inventory and matrix `SYNTHETIC / NOT LIVE EVIDENCE`.

## Prohibited outcome

Do not mark the exercise Complete, infer workforce decisions, or relabel a role-play as live because real professionals participated.

## Exit conditions

- Duty, legal/safety, and `auto_grant_minted: never` paths were exercised; gaps recorded.
- Real workforce material triggers stop/isolation and a separately consented LC intake.
- Registry remains **0 Complete** and RP-005 remains **Open**.
