# RP-005 Field Kit

**Program:** AI Workforce Transformation  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Base kit:** [FIELD_CAPTURE_KIT](../../../templates/FIELD_CAPTURE_KIT.md)

## Who

- [ ] Real workforce/domain observer, legal/compliance reviewer, safety specialist when physical actors are in scope, facilitator, and custodians.
- [ ] Worker/participant notice and consent plus T3 witness/attestor route if applicable.

## When / context

- [ ] Named workforce cohort/site and dated observation window.
- [ ] Role-framework, actor-taxonomy, risk/control, and legal-review versions.
- [ ] Included role families, processes, duties, actors, and exclusions.

## Systems / artifacts

- [ ] Authorized live role inventory and process/handoff/supervision sources.
- [ ] Outcomes/capabilities/duties/actor responsibility matrix, controls, residual human duties, and fusion vetoes.
- [ ] Exception/incident samples, integrity, provenance, retention, and falsifiers.

## Permissions / controls

- [ ] HR/labor/legal/safety approval for interviews, observation, exports, and incident use.
- [ ] Personal-data minimization, worker protections, redaction, and controlled retrieval.
- [ ] AI is not a legal person; Title/Role/Capability never mints grants.

This kit does not close [RP-005 gaps](GAP.md), mark Complete, flip a floor, Promote, open Eng work, or change Const/BP.
