# RP-005 Interview Plan

**Program:** AI Workforce Transformation  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Understand real Human/AI/Robot/Device duties, handoffs, controls, residual human accountability, and fusion vetoes without evaluating individuals or driving workforce action.

## Interviewee roles

1. Worker/domain practitioner performing in-scope duties.
2. Process/role owner or supervisor responsible for outcomes.
3. AI/tool/automation or Robot/Device operations representative.
4. Legal/compliance/labor/privacy reviewer.
5. Safety representative where physical actors are involved.

## Core questions

1. Walk through the last concrete case from input to outcome and handoffs.
2. Which duties are performed by Human, AI, Robot, or Device actors?
3. Which decisions and R1/R2 responsibilities must remain with accountable humans?
4. How are AI/automation outputs reviewed, overridden, escalated, and recovered?
5. Where do shadow tools, informal workarounds, or taxonomy confusion appear?
6. Which risks/controls permit or veto a proposed fusion?
7. How are physical safety, degraded mode, and emergency stop handled?
8. What happens when an actor or system is unavailable or wrong?
9. What evidence would falsify claimed efficiency, safety, or accountability benefits?
10. Which role, handoff, incident, supervision, and control records can be reviewed safely?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask participants to identify poor performers, redundancy targets, or replacement candidates.
2. Do not request unnecessary HR, health, compensation, discipline, or personal records.
3. Do not ask AI to be treated as legal person or responsibility holder.
4. Do not ask role/title/capability answers to create identity, Permission, or grants.

## Output mapping

1. Concrete-case answers → duty/handoff observation timeline.
2. Actor-duty answers → responsibility matrix.
3. Human-accountability answers → residual-duty/control record.
4. Shadow/exception answers → risk and workaround log.
5. Safety/fallback answers → veto/degraded-mode evidence map.
6. Falsifier/artifact answers → artifact requests, custody, and validation plan.

## Bias and follow-up

Include practitioners, not only managers/vendors; protect voluntariness and retaliation risk. Separate participant experience from policy claims and validate against approved process/control artifacts.
