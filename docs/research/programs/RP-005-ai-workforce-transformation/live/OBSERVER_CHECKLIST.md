# RP-005 Observer Checklist

**Program:** AI Workforce Transformation  
**Preparation status:** **Open**  
**Assigned observer:** none  

Use this checklist to prepare a real Human/AI/Robot/Device role-inventory observation. Observer identities are recorded only after actual participation and consent.

## Observer roles

- [ ] Lead observer understands the Human/AI/Robot/Device taxonomy and duty decomposition.
- [ ] Workforce/domain representative can validate real outcomes, duties, handoffs, and supervision.
- [ ] Legal/compliance observer can review accountability, residual human duty, and fusion vetoes.
- [ ] Safety specialist is present when physical Robot/Device duties are in scope.

## Site / system access

- [ ] Authorized cohort and dated observation window are defined.
- [ ] Read-only access covers role descriptions, process/handoff records, supervision controls, and relevant incidents.
- [ ] HR, labor, personal, safety, and regulated data access is minimized and approved.
- [ ] No credentials, personal records, or restricted operational payloads enter repository Markdown.

## Minimum artifacts

- [ ] Dated, redacted role inventory and cohort description.
- [ ] Outcomes/capabilities/duties/actor responsibility matrix.
- [ ] Risk, control, legal-review, supervision, and fusion-veto record.
- [ ] Handoff/exception samples, falsifiers, and provenance/retention metadata.

## Ethics / confidentiality

- [ ] Do not frame observation as workforce replacement, performance scoring, or disciplinary assessment.
- [ ] AI is not a legal person; residual human accountability is explicit.
- [ ] Respect worker notice, consent, representation, safety, and jurisdictional obligations.
- [ ] Title, role, and capability labels never grant permission.
