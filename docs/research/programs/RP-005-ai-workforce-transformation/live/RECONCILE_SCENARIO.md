## Mapping theme

Observe Human/AI/Robot/Device duties that maintain, compare, challenge, and hold parallel **SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR** facts. RP-005 maps accountability/supervision; no actor in this research executes commercial or reconciliation actions.

| Fact family | Workforce observation | Score / hold treatment |
|-------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| SO | Human/AI review of changes, terms, and exception evidence | HOLD if AI owns legal commitment |
| DO | Human/Robot/Device fulfillment/release duties and supervision | HOLD on unclear accountable release |
| Inventory | Device/system measurement plus human discrepancy control | Score fallback and confidence |
| Receipt | Human/device proof, discrepancy, escalation, and acceptance boundary | No accept-on-behalf |
| AR | Human/system matching, fraud/dispute review, and financial control | HOLD on automated settlement authority |

## RP-005 model treatment

1. Outcomes→duties→actor classes map to ANRF.
2. Residual R1/R2 human/legal responsibilities remain explicit.
3. Supervision, override, escalation, and fallback are scored from evidence.
4. Fusion eligibility is held on legal/safety/control gaps.
5. Title/role/capability never mints identity, Permission, or grant.

## Observable reconciliation checkpoints

1. SO changes are double-entered by humans and AI suggestions lack review provenance.
2. DO release duty is split across people/systems with no accountable final gate.
3. Inventory device/system fact diverges from physical count and fallback is unclear.
4. Receipt proof is captured by device/worker but acknowledgment authority is ambiguous.
5. AR matching automates happy paths while partial/disputed/chargeback cases lack human oversight.
6. Dual-write creates hidden labor, shadow spreadsheets, and error ownership gaps.
7. Reconciliation/勾兑 absence is compensated by undocumented worker judgment.
8. Exception paths transfer responsibility to AI/Robot/Device without legal accountability.
9. Supervision or override evidence is unavailable during degraded/system-unavailable states.

## Required live evidence

1. Dated/tokenized chain observation with actor/duty/handoff records.
2. SO/DO/inventory/Receipt/AR responsibility matrix.
3. Supervision, override, escalation, fallback, and approval artifacts.
4. Legal/compliance/labor/safety review and fusion veto evidence.
5. Naturally occurring dual-write/partial/dispute/degraded case.
6. Real practitioners/control owners corroborated by system/process records.
7. De-identified custody, minimization, integrity, retention, and falsifiers.
