# RP-005 Site Plan

**Program:** AI Workforce Transformation  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Assigned observer/site/window:** none / none / none  
**Access basis:** [SITE_ACCESS_PACK](../../../templates/SITE_ACCESS_PACK.md) · **Evidence review:** [ARTIFACT_ACCEPTANCE_RUBRIC](../../../templates/ARTIFACT_ACCEPTANCE_RUBRIC.md)

## Objective and boundary

Observe real role families as Human/AI/Robot/Device duty compositions while preserving legal accountability, supervision, safety, and Title/Role/Capability≠Permission.

## Target observation points

1. Outcomes and duties are identified before actor assignment or replacement discussion.
2. Human, AI, Robot, and Device actors are classified consistently.
3. Residual human R1/R2 responsibilities and accountable decision owners stay explicit.
4. Supervision, handoff, escalation, fallback, and exception paths are observable.
5. Risk classes and controls support or veto proposed fusion.
6. Shadow automation and undocumented AI/tool use are recorded without disciplinary framing.
7. Physical actor duties include safety, stop, degraded-mode, and maintenance constraints.
8. Role/title/capability labels never generate identity, permission, or grant.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated cohort/site scope and role-inventory session record.
2. Authorized role/process/handoff source inventory.
3. Outcomes-capabilities-duties-actor responsibility matrix.
4. Risk/control and residual-human-duty record.
5. Legal/compliance review and fusion-veto log.
6. Supervision, escalation, fallback, and exception samples.
7. Applicable safety/incident references for Robot/Device work.
8. Consent, redaction, provenance, integrity, retention, and falsifier manifest.

## Dependency systems / contexts

1. HR/role-description source under minimized read-only access.
2. Process/workflow/handoff evidence source.
3. AI/tool usage and supervision record source.
4. Robot/device/safety system context when physical actors are included.
5. Legal/compliance control repository.
6. Secure role-mapping workspace.

## Access and run sequence

1. Approve worker notice/consent, labor/legal/safety review, cohort, systems, and prohibited uses.
2. Observe work/duties without evaluating individual performance.
3. Validate actor assignments, controls, vetoes, and residual duty with authorized representatives.
4. Redact identities, reconcile artifacts, and revoke temporary access.

## Risks / ethics

1. Workforce surveillance, retaliation, or performance scoring; prohibit and minimize individual data.
2. Replacement narrative coercing participants; frame as duty/risk research with optional participation.
3. AI treated as legal person or accountability sink; preserve accountable humans/legal entities.
4. Safety-critical Robot/Device observation exceeding research competence; require safety authority and stop.
5. Shadow automation disclosure creating security/legal exposure; use controlled escalation.
6. Role/title becoming grant input; `auto_grant_minted: never`.

## Exit conditions

1. Stop if worker notice/consent, labor/legal basis, or safety authority is absent/revoked.
2. Stop if observation risks production, physical safety, retaliation, or unauthorized surveillance.
3. Stop if requested use includes hiring, discipline, mass replacement, permission, or grant decisions.
4. Return incomplete if responsibility, control, or source evidence cannot be retained safely.
5. RP-005 remains Open; no site activity or rubric score alone marks Complete.
