## Research purpose

Trace the infrastructure/control-plane evidence supporting an existing commercial chain, emphasizing identity, approval bridges, observability, isolation, provenance, degraded mode, and fail-closed boundaries.

## Chain observation points

1. **Sample:** channel/identity landing, product data source, request audit, and tenant boundary.
2. **Quote:** model/tool assistance, approval bridge, version/provenance, and confidential data handling.
3. **Order:** identity/authority enforcement, workflow evidence, integration reliability, and duplicate control.
4. **Shipment:** IT/OT/warehouse/carrier integration, event provenance, degraded mode, and replay.
5. **Receipt/invoice:** event correlation, billing integration, audit, and exception observability.
6. **Payment:** provider integration, segregation, security/fraud signals, reconciliation, and retention.
7. **Cross-stage:** correlation IDs, clocks, telemetry gaps, tenant isolation, and supply-chain trust.
8. **Failure:** outage/degradation/queue retry without bypass or hidden mutation.

## RP model mapping

1. Identity and tenant evidence maps to AIRM identity/isolation domains.
2. Model/tool/connector versions map to signed supply-chain readiness.
3. Approval/workflow bridges map to fail-closed control readiness.
4. Correlation/audit/metrics map to observability and FinOps readiness.
5. Shipment/edge degradation maps to OT/degraded-mode readiness.
