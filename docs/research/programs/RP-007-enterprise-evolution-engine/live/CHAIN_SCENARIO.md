# RP-007 Commercial Chain Scenario

**Program:** Enterprise Evolution Engine  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Live chain/observer assigned:** none / none  
**Protocol:** [COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION](../../../templates/COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION.md) · **Terminal lens:** [TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD](../../../templates/TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD.md)

## Research purpose

Use observed chain evidence/exceptions to test advisory triggers, recommendations, simulations, HOLD, human decision, and learning without allowing the Evolution Engine to act.

## Chain observation points

1. **Sample:** repeated delays/specification gaps as possible evidence signals, not automatic triggers.
2. **Quote:** approval bottlenecks, margin/term exceptions, and stale knowledge evidence.
3. **Order:** change/cancel/hold patterns, compliance/credit exceptions, and demand volatility.
4. **Shipment:** capacity, inventory, logistics, safety/trade exceptions, and simulation need.
5. **Receipt/invoice:** dispute, damage, billing mismatch, and control/reconciliation evidence.
6. **Payment:** failed/late/unapplied patterns, fraud/control signals, and financial HOLD.
7. **Cross-stage:** provenance/freshness of trigger evidence and competing explanations.
8. **Decision:** human accept/defer/reject/HOLD and later outcome evidence.

## RP model mapping

1. Chain events map to evidence-backed trigger candidates.
2. REC-ORG/AI/AUTO/ROBOT/CAP/TERM/HOLD classify bounded advisory options.
3. Cross-stage scenarios support explain/simulate with declared assumptions.
4. Human decisions map to immutable recommendation lifecycle.
5. Later chain outcomes support learning/falsification without rewriting history.

## HARD HOLD / prohibited zones

1. HARD HOLD if a signal lacks provenance/freshness or could have competing operational causes.
2. HARD HOLD if recommendations are sent as commands, approvals, customer messages, or transaction changes.
3. HARD HOLD if accept/review is treated as execution, grant, or Twin authorization.
4. HARD HOLD on Brain execute, Terminal production connection, Promote/Eng opening, or Const/BP change.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated/tokenized commercial-chain observation log.
2. Trigger-to-chain-event/source matrix.
3. Recommendation/HOLD ledger with rationale/confidence.
4. Simulation assumptions/results and alternatives.
5. Human accept/defer/reject/request-evidence record.
6. Tool/side-effect audit proving no transaction action.
7. Outcome/falsifier, custody, integrity, and retention record.

## Terminal research lens

A card may show advisory trigger evidence, simulation, alternatives, and HOLD/review choices. All choices are research-only and produce no commercial side effect.
