# RP-007 Site Plan

**Program:** Enterprise Evolution Engine  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Assigned observer/context/window:** none / none / none  
**Access basis:** [SITE_ACCESS_PACK](../../../templates/SITE_ACCESS_PACK.md) · **Evidence review:** [ARTIFACT_ACCEPTANCE_RUBRIC](../../../templates/ARTIFACT_ACCEPTANCE_RUBRIC.md)

## Objective and boundary

Observe advisory evolution against a real frozen dossier, including deliberate HOLD cases and human decisions. Recommendations never execute, authorize Twin, bypass Permission/Workflow, or become Eng instructions.

## Target observation points

1. Evaluate consumes versioned Discovery/role/capability inputs with explicit freshness.
2. Triggers trace to evidence, thresholds, assumptions, and counter-evidence.
3. REC-ORG/AI/AUTO/ROBOT/CAP/TERM and HOLD remain distinguishable.
4. Explanation/simulation communicates uncertainty and alternative outcomes.
5. Human reviewers accept, defer, reject, or request evidence without hidden execution.
6. Independent usefulness scoring compares against a declared checklist baseline.
7. Should-HOLD cases remain HOLD under sponsor pressure.
8. Recommendation learning records outcome evidence without retroactive rewriting.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated decision-context scope and frozen real-dossier manifest.
2. Trigger evaluation with evidence/threshold trace.
3. Recommendation/HOLD ledger with class, rationale, confidence, and provenance.
4. Explanation and simulation outputs with model/protocol versions.
5. Blind usefulness-scoring rubric and results.
6. Human accept/defer/reject/request-evidence record.
7. Side-effect/tool-call audit proving advisory boundary.
8. Exceptions, dissent, falsifiers, integrity, retention, and custodian manifest.

## Dependency systems / contexts

1. Controlled Discovery/DNA/capability/workforce dossier source.
2. Trigger evaluation workspace.
3. Isolated simulation/analysis environment.
4. Recommendation ledger/review workspace.
5. Human decision/attestation record system.
6. Audit/log source for zero-side-effect verification.

## Access and run sequence

1. Approve dossier, reviewers, simulation environment, decision observation, and no-execution boundary.
2. Freeze inputs, triggers, rubric, and protocol versions.
3. Observe evaluation/recommendation and collect independent decisions/scores.
4. Reconcile ledger/audit, revoke access, and keep operational decisions outside Research.

## Risks / ethics

1. Advice perceived as mandate; display advisory status and accountable human decision.
2. Sponsor pressure suppressing HOLD or dissent; preserve blind scores and adverse evidence.
3. Sensitive strategy/workforce/robot investment disclosure; minimize and control.
4. Simulation uncertainty hidden by polished output; require assumptions and limitations.
5. Accept click interpreted as authorization or execution; prohibit and audit.
6. Learning step rewriting prior evidence; preserve immutable decision history.

## Exit conditions

1. Stop if input freeze, decision-context consent, or reviewer independence cannot be established.
2. Stop if a recommendation requests or triggers a mutating action, grant, workflow commit, or Twin authorization.
3. Stop if should-HOLD evidence is suppressed or the rubric changes after results.
4. Return incomplete if decision, provenance, or zero-side-effect artifacts are unavailable.
5. RP-007 remains Open; no recommendation quality score alone marks Complete.
