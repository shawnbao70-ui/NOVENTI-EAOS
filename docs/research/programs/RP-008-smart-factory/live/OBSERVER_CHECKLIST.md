# RP-008 Observer Checklist

**Program:** Smart Factory  
**Preparation status:** **Open**  
**Assigned observer:** none  

This checklist prepares an observational plant capture only. It does not authorize entry, production interference, safety testing, machine control, or MES change.

## Observer roles

- [ ] Lead observer understands SF-01…08 and PR0–PR4.
- [ ] Plant/process representative can validate cell, line, event, and operational context.
- [ ] Authorized safety stakeholder can stop observation and validate safety boundaries/vetoes.
- [ ] OT/terminal observer understands degraded/offline modes and the prohibition on Brain machine control.

## Site / system access

- [ ] Site induction, escort, PPE, zone, photography/recording, and dated-window approvals are complete.
- [ ] Any OT/MES/historian access is read-only or supervised and explicitly scoped.
- [ ] Observation cannot change machine state, safety controls, schedules, recipes, or production data.
- [ ] Incident, worker, trade-secret, and critical-infrastructure handling rules are documented.

## Minimum artifacts

- [ ] Dated, redacted plant/cell scope and walkthrough record.
- [ ] SF domain/risk assessment, event/process flow, and actor-duty matrix.
- [ ] Safety-case/approval/veto and degraded-mode references.
- [ ] Source-backed OEE/quality/maintenance notes, falsifiers, and provenance metadata.

## Ethics / confidentiality

- [ ] Safety and production continuity override research collection.
- [ ] Do not identify workers or evaluate individual performance.
- [ ] Keep OT topology, vulnerabilities, recipes, and production payloads controlled.
- [ ] Report near misses or safety concerns through plant procedures, not public research notes.
