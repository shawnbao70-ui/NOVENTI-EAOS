## 1. Thesis

Industry “enterprise copilots” and KPI dashboards routinely blur **advice** and **control**. Without explicit insight classes, provenance, and anti-execution red team, Brain evolution will be sold as Act under other names. BEM keeps Describe→Learn advisory-only; AE-01…03 falsify quiet triggers.
