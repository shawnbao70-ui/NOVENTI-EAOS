Define insight classes, simulation depth, provenance, Twin coupling, and anti-execution guarantees for Brain evolution — never Act.

## 2. Business Background

Brain exists as advisory-only. Discovery/Evolution maturity requires more interpretive power without operational authority.

## 3. Industry Problems

Analytics quietly trigger actions; copilots blur advice vs commit; Twin becomes dashboard-only; weak provenance.
