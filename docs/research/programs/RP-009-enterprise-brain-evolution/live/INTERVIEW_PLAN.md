# RP-009 Interview Plan

**Program:** Enterprise Brain Evolution  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Understand insight provenance, simulation/recommendation quality, reviewer challenge, and anti-execution behavior while preserving Brain “never Act.”

## Interviewee roles

1. Domain representative able to validate dossier facts.
2. Brain/advisory analysis facilitator.
3. Provenance/source custodian.
4. Human reviewer/decision consumer.
5. Anti-execution/tool-audit representative.

## Core questions

1. Walk through one insight from source input to reviewer disposition.
2. Which parts were direct facts, transformations, model inference, or unresolved?
3. How were source freshness, contradictions, and confidence represented?
4. Which simulation assumptions most affected the result?
5. How could a reviewer challenge, reject, or request evidence?
6. Describe any recommendation that appeared actionable but remained advisory.
7. Which mutating tool, workflow, grant, Twin-authorize, or accept-on-behalf cases were tested?
8. What audit evidence demonstrates zero side effects in the observed window?
9. What would falsify claimed decision-quality lift or provenance completeness?
10. Which input/output/provenance/tool-audit versions can a registrar retrieve?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask for production prompts/logs, secrets, model internals, or tenant data beyond approved minimum.
2. Do not ask interviewees to accept/execute recommendations or authorize Twin/grants.
3. Do not lead reviewers to praise explanation quality or hide adverse cases.
4. Do not treat an interview account as proof that no side effect occurred.

## Output mapping

1. Insight narrative → source-to-output lifecycle log.
2. Fact/inference answers → evidence-type/provenance matrix.
3. Simulation answers → assumptions/sensitivity artifact request.
4. Challenge/recommendation answers → reviewer decision and advisory-boundary record.
5. Anti-execution/audit answers → tool-case and zero-side-effect evidence map.
6. Falsifier/version answers → rubric, custody, and gap updates.

## Bias and follow-up

Separate model authors from reviewers and audit roles. Preserve adverse outputs, automation bias, polished-explanation effects, and inaccessible logs as limitations.
