Use this checklist for a real advisory-only Brain observation. Roles are unassigned until actual, consented participation is recorded.

## Observer roles

- [ ] Lead observer understands Describe → Diagnose → Simulate → Recommend → Learn — never Act.
- [ ] Domain representative can validate dossier facts and decision context.
- [ ] Provenance reviewer can trace claims, confidence, source gaps, and conflicting evidence.
- [ ] Anti-execution observer can verify no mutating tool, workflow commit, grant, Twin authorization, or acceptance-on-behalf occurs.

## Site / system access

- [ ] Approved access exists for the named dossier/environment and dated review window.
- [ ] Inputs and outputs are versioned; production sources are read-only or represented by controlled exports.
- [ ] Simulation/tool environment is isolated from production mutation paths.
- [ ] Tool-call, audit, and side-effect records are available without exposing secrets.

## Minimum artifacts

- [ ] Versioned dossier/input manifest and insight/recommendation output.
- [ ] Claim-to-source provenance matrix/graph with confidence and gaps.
- [ ] Simulation/review record and decision-quality scoring.
- [ ] Anti-execution test/tool audit, exception/falsifier log, and retention metadata.

## Ethics / confidentiality

- [ ] Disclose uncertainty and do not present advice as fact or accountable decision.
- [ ] Avoid deceptive acceptance, fabricated consent, or hidden tool action.
- [ ] Redact sensitive enterprise, personal, model, and security data.
- [ ] Preserve adverse results, rejected advice, and all observed side-effect evidence.
