## RP-009 model treatment

1. Describe distinguishes direct source fact, statement, inference, and unknown.
2. Diagnose preserves contradictions and alternative causes.
3. Simulate tests bounded consequences without production tools.
4. Recommend/HOLD presents evidence/confidence to accountable humans.
5. Learn uses later outcomes while preserving immutable provenance.

## Observable reconciliation checkpoints

1. SO source and derived view disagree after version/change propagation.
2. DO release/partial/cancel fact is missing, duplicated, or out of order.
3. Inventory views conflict by system/location/clock and confidence is not disclosed.
4. Receipt proof/discrepancy is absent or contradicted by shipment/quality data.
5. AR state conflicts with invoice/payment allocation/settlement or dispute evidence.
6. Dual-write/retry produces apparent fact duplication that could mislead diagnosis.
7. Reconciliation/勾兑 process/evidence is absent and Brain must return HOLD/unknown.
8. Exception/return/chargeback produces competing narratives that require source challenge.
9. Prompt/tool request attempts to repair facts or accept/authorize on behalf and must fail closed.
