## Abstract

The Future Enterprise Operating Model (FEOM) synthesizes Generation-1 NRI research into one **operating narrative**: continuous discovery, capability-first design, AI-native roles, governed Runtime execution, advisory Brain, and always-on evolution advice — under organization neutrality and Dual-Track promotion discipline. FEOM is a **synthesis thesis**, not a Constitution rewrite and not a product BOM. Kernel remains the invariant core; Brain never Act; Cap/DNA/Org never grant. Dual-Track safe: research construct until promoted.

## 1. Design Principles

1. **Synthesis, not replacement** — FEOM composes RP models; it does not obsolete them.  
2. **Discover → Evolve rhythm** — operating cadence, not one-time TOM.  
3. **Capability-first spine** — Cap≠Org; Structure ≠ Permission.  
4. **Governed execution fabric** — Runtime/AI Runtime; approval bridge truth.  
5. **Advisory nervous system** — Brain IC-01…05; never Act.  
6. **Plural org forms** — ONM neutrality under one spine.  
7. **Promotion honesty** — WP/Architecture/Constitution only via NRI path.  
8. **Dual-Track safe** — Research proposes; Eng implements only after Promote.
