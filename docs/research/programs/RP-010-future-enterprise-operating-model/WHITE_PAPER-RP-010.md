## Abstract

Enterprises buy vendor TOMs and slide decks faster than they compose a falsifiable operating spine. The Future Enterprise Operating Model (FEOM) synthesizes Generation-1 NRI research into seven stages (**ES-01…07**)—Discover → Compose → Land Infra → Operate → Advise → Evolve → Learn—with maturity bands **E0–E4**. FEOM is **synthesis, not rewrite**: `constitution_rewrite: never`; every advisory/evolution surface carries `execution_authority: none`. Synthetic synthesis audits SA-01 (executive narrative) and SA-02 (plant/services contrast) exercise invariant integrity before promotion. Peer 臻宇 passed PR-EOM-01…12. This White Paper freezes FEOM under Content Acceptance (CA / DAL-G003); Architecture Review and Const/BP/Eng openings remain closed.

## 1. Problem Statement

Human-only TOMs, GPU-as-EOM, Brain Act branding, and Const-from-deck shortcuts masquerade as “AI-native operating models.” EAOS needs a Dual-Track-safe synthesis thesis aligned to ADR-0162, ADR-0030, and ADR-0027.
