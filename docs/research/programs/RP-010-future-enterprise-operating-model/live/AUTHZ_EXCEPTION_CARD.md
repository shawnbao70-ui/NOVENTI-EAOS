## Field objective

Observe, score, and HOLD how authorization exceptions are owned, reviewed, expired, reconciled, and governed across an operating model. This research does not prescribe or activate a future authority model.

## Authorization / bypass observation points

1. Map decision rights for policy ownership, access grant, approval, override, review, and revocation.
2. Observe separation between UI visibility, module permission, object/tenant scope, and command authorization.
3. Trace exception intake, reason, risk, approver, duration, expiry, compensating control, and closure.
4. Record Admin, support, service-account, emergency, delegated, and cross-unit bypass governance.
5. Compare canonical/residual routes, integrations, manual workarounds, and shared accounts for policy consistency.
6. Observe periodic access review, joiner/mover/leaver, stale entitlement, and orphaned identity disposition.
7. Capture denial, escalation, incident linkage, audit evidence, exception metrics, and accountable sign-off.

## Scoring / HOLD

- Score decision-right clarity, scope, segregation, exception lifecycle, monitoring, and accountable closure.
- Dossier local operating variants; do not convert current workarounds into target governance.
- HOLD when role labels, administrator convention, or policy documents lack runtime decision evidence.

## Required live evidence

1. Authorized redacted decision-right and authorization-policy ownership map.
2. Allow/deny traces tied to principal, role, tenant, object, action, and policy version.
3. Real exception records showing reason, approver, duration, control, expiry, review, and closure.
4. Access-review and joiner/mover/leaver artifacts, including stale entitlement disposition.
5. Audit/incident/metric evidence for denials, bypasses, and unchanged-state outcomes.
6. Custodian corroboration, custody, minimization, retention, contradictions, and falsifiers.

Missing evidence keeps RP-010 **Open / 0 Complete**.
