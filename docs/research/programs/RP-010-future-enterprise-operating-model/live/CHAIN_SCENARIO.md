# RP-010 Commercial Chain Scenario

**Program:** Future Enterprise Operating Model  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Live chain/observer assigned:** none / none  
**Protocol:** [COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION](../../../templates/COMMERCIAL_CHAIN_OBSERVATION.md) · **Terminal lens:** [TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD](../../../templates/TERMINAL_SCENARIO_CARD.md)

## Research purpose

Use one bounded commercial chain to test coherence of ES-01…07 across discovery, capability/workforce composition, infrastructure, operation, advice, evolution, and learning—without turning synthesis into implementation.

## Chain observation points

1. **Sample:** discovery of customer/product/context evidence and capability/workforce composition.
2. **Quote:** governed knowledge, pricing/terms decision rights, infrastructure, and accountability.
3. **Order:** acceptance, compliance, capability ownership, workflow, and human decision.
4. **Shipment:** Runtime/operational fabric, plant/warehouse/logistics actors, safety, and exception handling.
5. **Receipt/invoice:** evidence, dispute, billing/tax controls, and cross-system reconciliation.
6. **Payment:** financial control, settlement/reconciliation, and accountable closure.
7. **Cross-stage:** continuous evidence/provenance, neutrality, Kernel/Permission boundaries, and learning.
8. **Evolution:** chain gaps generate advisory options/HOLD and staged migration—not automatic change.

## RP model mapping

1. Chain evidence and dossier map to **ES-01 Discover**.
2. Capability/organization/workforce duties map to **ES-02 Compose**.
3. Identity/integration/observability map to **ES-03 Land Infra** and **ES-04 Operate**.
4. Brain/Terminal review maps to **ES-05 Advise** without Act.
5. Human decisions/outcomes map to **ES-06 Evolve** and **ES-07 Learn** under HOLD.

## HARD HOLD / prohibited zones

1. HARD HOLD if one chain is generalized into enterprise-wide EOM without declared sampling limits/corroboration.
2. HARD HOLD if executive/title/Terminal interaction substitutes for operational evidence or authorization.
3. HARD HOLD on any transaction action, workforce action, Brain execute, Twin authorize, or grant mint.
4. HARD HOLD on architecture approval, Promote, Eng opening, Const/BP rewrite, or software-shaped forced reorganization.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated/tokenized end-to-end chain observation log.
2. ES-01…07 stage/evidence trace matrix.
3. Cross-RP capability/workforce/infra/Brain/neutrality consistency audit.
4. High-impact decision/control/HOLD map.
5. Exception/degraded/dispute/falsifier evidence.
6. Human review/migration/dependency record.
7. Custody, minimization, integrity, consent, retention, and provenance manifest.

## Terminal research lens

A future card may present the chain as a read-only EOM walkthrough with evidence, role intent, accountable gate, and HOLD. It is a research narrative—not a product/API requirement.
