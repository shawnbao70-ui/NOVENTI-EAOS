# RP-010 Custody Plan

**Program:** Future Enterprise Operating Model  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Controls:** [DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK](../../../templates/DATA_MINIMIZATION_PACK.md) · [CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY](../../../templates/CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY.md)

## Scope

Controls enterprise dossiers, RP-001…009 references, ES-01…07 traces, consistency/neutrality/legal audits, migration analyses, executive review/attestation, dissent, and board-material handles under the [SITE_PLAN](SITE_PLAN.md) and [FIELD_KIT](FIELD_KIT.md).

## Custody nodes

1. **Enterprise/board source:** strategy, legal, financial, workforce, and operating owners retain restricted originals.
2. **Cross-RP intake:** collector registers frozen dossier and RP reference versions through minimum controlled handles.
3. **Synthesis workspace:** ES-01…07 trace and audit derivatives link to exact parent versions.
4. **Executive review:** attestations, corrections, dissent, and migration concerns append without rewriting source or granting authority.
5. **Board/redaction review:** custodian/reviewer validates restricted strategy, identity, legal, financial, and stakeholder handling.
6. **Registrar/disposition:** registrar reviews controlled evidence; temporary board/dossier extracts are returned/deleted and safe provenance retained.

## Responsibility roles

1. Enterprise/board/source owners approve source scope, versions, participants, and retention.
2. Synthesis collector records evidence and dissent without converting review into approval.
3. Evidence custodian controls restricted workspace, access, versions, transfers, and retention.
4. Neutrality/legal/redaction reviewer validates cross-RP transformations and sensitive-content handling.
5. Registrar assesses claimed tier independently; executive title/signature is not sufficient.

## Retention rules

1. Raw board, strategy, legal, financial, workforce, and dossier records remain in owner-controlled stores.
2. Temporary redacted extracts and working synthesis copies expire after executive/registrar review or access expiry.
3. Safe ES trace, audit outcomes, attestation metadata, dissent, manifests, versions, and provenance follow the research record schedule.
4. Legal hold, correction, or attestation dispute preserves only affected versions; original and superseding records remain linked.

## Leakage response

1. Stop review/sharing and isolate board handles, dossier extracts, synthesis workspace, and attestations.
2. Notify source/board owner, custodian, privacy/security/legal and accountable session contacts.
3. Trace exposed strategy, legal, financial, workforce, personal, dissent, decision, derivative, and backup content.
4. Revoke access, quarantine/rebuild derivatives, verify return/deletion, preserve dispute history, and exclude evidence.
