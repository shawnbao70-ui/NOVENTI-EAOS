6. Verify Kernel invariance, advisory-only Brain, synthesis-not-rewrite, and no software-shaped org coercion.

## Fake-data boundary

- Use invented enterprises, executives, strategy, finances, workforce, systems, and operating outcomes.
- No real board deck, executive statement, dossier, legal advice, financial record, or restricted strategy.
- Label all narratives and attestations `SYNTHETIC / NOT LIVE EVIDENCE`.

## Prohibited outcome

A simulated executive review, consensus, or signature cannot be marked Complete or used as T3 attestation.

## Exit conditions

- ES-01…07 and synthesis boundary checks were exercised; gaps and owners recorded.
- Real board/executive/enterprise evidence triggers stop/isolation and separate authorized LC intake.
- Registry remains **0 Complete** and RP-010 remains **Open**.
