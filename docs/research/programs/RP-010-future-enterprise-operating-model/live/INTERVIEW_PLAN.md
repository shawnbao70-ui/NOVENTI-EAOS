# RP-010 Interview Plan

**Program:** Future Enterprise Operating Model  
**Status:** **Open** · **0 Complete**  
**Participants/interviewers assigned:** none  
**Protocol:** [INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL](../../../templates/INTERVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) · **Log:** [OBSERVATION_LOG](../../../templates/OBSERVATION_LOG.md)

## Purpose

Elicit evidence-backed executive/domain assessment of ES-01…07 coherence, migration feasibility, neutrality, legal responsibility, and advisory boundaries without turning discussion into approval or rewrite.

## Interviewee roles

1. Enterprise executive/sponsor with strategic context.
2. Operations/domain representative affected by the model.
3. Technology/infrastructure/runtime representative.
4. Workforce/legal/neutrality reviewer.
5. Independent synthesis/boundary reviewer.

## Core questions

1. Walk through how the current enterprise context maps—or fails to map—to ES-01…07.
2. Which RP-001…009 inputs are directly supported, contradictory, or missing?
3. Where does the synthesis assume an organization form that does not fit?
4. How are Human/AI/Robot/Device responsibilities and accountable decisions represented?
5. Which migration stage is feasible now, and which dependency requires HOLD?
6. Where might the narrative force software-shaped organization redesign?
7. How are Brain advice, human decision, Runtime execution, and Kernel/Permission boundaries separated?
8. Which stakeholder perspective is absent or likely to disagree?
9. What operational evidence would falsify the proposed end-state or migration path?
10. Which dossier, trace, audit, review, and attestation records can be retained/reviewed?

## Taboo questions

1. Do not ask executives to approve architecture, Promote, implementation, workforce action, or Const/BP rewrite.
2. Do not request unrestricted board, legal, financial, workforce, or strategy material.
3. Do not treat title, consensus, or signature as operational evidence or T3 by itself.
4. Do not suppress dissent or ask participants to align with the sponsor narrative.

## Output mapping

1. ES mapping answers → ES-01…07 trace matrix.
2. Cross-RP conflict answers → consistency/contradiction audit.
3. Neutrality/responsibility answers → neutrality/legal audit.
4. Migration/HOLD answers → dependency and staged-migration record.
5. Missing-stakeholder/falsifier answers → gap and challenge plan.
6. Reviewable-record answers → attestation/custody/provenance manifest.

## Bias and follow-up

Balance executive framing with operations, workforce/legal, technology, and independent reviewers. Record sponsor authority effects, consensus pressure, absent stakeholders, and differences between narrative agreement and operational evidence.
