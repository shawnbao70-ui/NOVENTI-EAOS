## Objective and boundary

Observe whether ES-01…07 provides a coherent, comprehensible operating-model synthesis for a real enterprise context. The session is narrative/tabletop research, not Const/BP rewrite, architecture approval, or implementation authorization.

## Target observation points

1. Discover → Compose → Land Infra → Operate → Advise → Evolve → Learn traces to real context evidence.
2. RP-001…009 inputs remain internally consistent and conflicts are visible.
3. Organization neutrality and capability-first design survive executive simplification.
4. Human/AI/Robot/Device legal responsibilities and supervision remain explicit.
5. Brain/Evolution stay advisory and Kernel/Permission boundaries remain invariant.
6. Executives understand migration stages, dependencies, HOLD points, and uncertainty.
7. Disagreement, infeasible transitions, missing evidence, and affected stakeholder views are retained.
8. The operating narrative avoids forcing software-shaped organization redesign.

## Required artifacts

1. Dated executive/tabletop scope, agenda, cohort, and consent record.
2. Authorized versioned real enterprise dossier.
3. ES-01…07 trace matrix to RP-001…009 source references.
4. Cross-program consistency and contradiction audit.
5. Organization-neutrality and legal-responsibility audit.
6. Migration stage/dependency/HOLD analysis.
7. Executive review/attestation, disagreement, and decision notes.
8. Provenance, integrity, redaction, access, retention, gaps, and falsifier manifest.

## Dependency systems / contexts

1. Controlled enterprise dossier/strategy repository.
2. RP-001…009 evidence/reference library.
3. EOM narrative and ES-01…07 trace workspace.
4. Consistency/neutrality/legal audit workspace.

## Access and run sequence

1. Approve executive cohort, board/strategy source access, recording, attestation, and retention.
2. Freeze dossier, RP references, EOM version, and review instruments.
3. Facilitate walkthrough while separately recording evidence, interpretation, dissent, and gaps.
4. Reconcile restricted artifacts, attestations, corrections, and access revocation.

## Risks / ethics

1. Executive authority substituting for operational evidence; require source trace and corroboration.
2. Sponsor consensus suppressing dissent/affected stakeholders; preserve contrary views.
3. Board, strategy, financial, workforce, or legal confidentiality leakage; minimize and control.
4. Narrative used to justify forced reorganization or workforce action; prohibit implementation inference.
5. Synthesis drifting into Const/BP normative rewrite; keep research proposal-only.
6. Attestation treated as T3 by title alone; require dated operational artifacts and provenance.

## Exit conditions

1. Stop if board/source access, participant consent, recording, or attestation basis is absent/revoked.
2. Stop if the session is redirected into approval, implementation, workforce action, or Const/BP rewrite.
3. Stop if source trace, cross-RP version, or confidentiality control cannot be maintained.
4. Return incomplete if operational corroboration or independent review required by the claimed tier is missing.
5. RP-010 remains Open; executive attendance, signature, or rubric score alone never marks Complete.
